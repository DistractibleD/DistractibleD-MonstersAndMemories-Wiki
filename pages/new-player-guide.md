# New Player Guide

A quick primer for brand-new adventurers in Aêthoril — enough to get you moving, fighting,
and questing. (Condensed from the [official new player guide](https://monstersandmemories.com/new-player-guide) — see that page for pictures and animated examples.)

## Inventory & Gear

- Press **I** to open your inventory — stats on the left, bags and equipment on the right.
- You can carry 1 backpack, 3 bags, and 3 belt items.
- Hold right-click over an item to inspect it; **Alt + left-click** shows its full
  description.
- Your starting candle lights up the dark (right-click to toggle) but won't last forever —
  buy a torch or lantern from a merchant once it's out.

## Spells & Abilities

- New characters start with a few ability scrolls in their inventory.
- Press **B** to open your spellbook. It must be equipped in a belt slot to *scribe* or
  *memorize* new spells — not to cast ones you already know.
- Left-click a scroll to pick it up, then click an empty spellbook square to scribe it (or
  just right-click the scroll to quick-scribe it).
- Drag up to 10 memorized abilities onto your ability bar, then trigger them with the **1-0**
  number keys.

## Combat

- **Tab** targets the nearest enemy; **C** "considers" it, giving you a sense of how tough it
  is compared to you (shown as a color).
- Press **Q** to start auto-attacking in melee. Ranged classes (like Archers) need a
  Shoot/Throw ability instead.
- Right-click a defeated enemy's corpse to loot it.
- Press **P** any time to check your current skill levels.

## Talking to NPCs & Quests

- Target an NPC and press **H** to "hail" them and start a conversation.
- Reply using whatever keywords they mention, typed as normal chat — don't start
  auto-attacking while you're talking to someone!
- Press **J** to open your Journal and re-read past NPC conversations.
- To hand over a quest item, left-click it to pick it up, then left-click the NPC.
- No quest log exists — quests are discovered and tracked entirely through conversation, so
  the Journal is your best reference.
- Some quests require MUD commands instead, like `/pick up item` or similar. For spoilers,
  see the [Useful Links](#useful-links.md) page.

## Grouping Up

- Groups share experience, coin, and corpse-looting rights.
- Type `/lfg on` to flag yourself as looking for a group, or open the **Group Finder**
  (press **O**, then the Group Finder tab) to browse or post listings.
- Group level range: everyone must be within 5 levels of the highest member until level 15,
  and within two-thirds of their level after that.

## Death

- Dying drops your items onto your corpse and sends you back to your bind point. Some
  classes can bind you to a new location.
- Return to your corpse to loot it back, or get help from another player (some spells/classes
  can locate or resurrect corpses, restoring some lost experience).
- `/consent <name>` lets a trusted player drag your corpse for you; `/deny` revokes it.
- Corpses expire (disappear for good) after 30 minutes to 7 days depending on level.

## Resting & States

- Press **X** to sit. Sitting increases your health and mana regeneration. Some classes
  learn the Meditation skill, which further increases mana regeneration while sitting.
- Sitting near a campfire or inside an inn puts you in a **Rested** state — worth a 15%
  experience bonus once fully built up.
- **Hungry/Thirsty** slows health and mana regen — keep food and drink stocked.
- **Encumbered** (carrying too much weight) slows your movement and blocks
  teleporting/gating — don't over-fill your bags.

## Useful Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/help [term]` | List commands, optionally searching for one |
| `/bug` or `/petition` | Report a problem or request help |
| `/stuck` | Frees you if stuck in the scenery (5 min cooldown) |
| `/camp` | Logs you out safely after a 30-second timer |
| `/random <dice>` | Rolls dice, e.g. `/random 1d20` |
| `/hidecorpse <option>` | Hides corpses cluttering the ground |
| `/ignore <name>` | Blocks messages from a player |

## Glossary

Common shorthand you'll see in chat:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Aggro | Getting a hostile NPC's attention |
| AOE | Area of effect — hits multiple targets |
| Bind | Where you respawn after death |
| Buff / Debuff | A spell that helps / hurts a target |
| Camp | A spot where a group parks and fights repeated spawns |
| CC | Crowd control — stalling an enemy's attack |
| Con | "Consider" — checking how tough an NPC is |
| Corpse Run (CR) | Traveling back to recover your corpse |
| DD / DoT | Direct damage / damage over time |
| Ding | Leveling up |
| EXP / XP | Experience points |
| Gate | A spell that teleports you to your Bind point |
| GM | Game Master (official staff) |
| HP / MP | Hit points / mana points |
| Kite | Fighting while staying out of an enemy's attack range |
| KOS | "Kill on sight" — an NPC that attacks on approach |
| KS | "Kill steal" — stealing credit for a kill |
| LD | "Link dead" — disconnected |
| LFG / LFM | Looking for group / looking for more |
| Mez | Temporarily incapacitating a target |
| Mob | Any monster or NPC |
| Named | A uniquely-named, tougher NPC with better loot |
| Nerf | A strength reduction to a player/item/ability |
| No Drop / No Rent | Can't be traded or dropped / disappears on logout |
| NPC / PC | Non-player character / player character |
| OOC | Out of character — casual zone-wide chat |
| OOM | Out of mana |
| PH | "Placeholder" — a common spawn that can turn into a Named |
| Proc | An effect that triggers randomly |
| Pull | Luring an enemy back to your camp |
| PVP | Player versus player |
| Res / Rez | Resurrect |
| Root / Snare | Prevents movement / slows movement |
| Train | Multiple hostile mobs chasing a player toward others |
| TS | Tradeskill |
| Unique | An item you can only hold/equip one of at a time |
| WTB / WTS / WTT | Want to buy / sell / trade |
| Zone / Zoning | An area of the world / traveling between areas |
