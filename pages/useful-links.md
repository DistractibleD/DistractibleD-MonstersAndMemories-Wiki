# Useful Links

- **[Official website](https://monstersandmemories.com/)** — the official Monsters and Memories site.
- **[monstersandmemories.miraheze.org](https://monstersandmemories.miraheze.org/wiki/Main_Page)** — a fan-run wiki that has some outdated info, but often gets updates and submissions — might be the best place to find information about the game.
- **[MnM Quest](https://mnmquest.com/)** — a very good resource for quest information and quest dialogue. This site is AI-powered, using information pulled from the unofficial wiki above and from Reddit.
- **[MnM Drops](https://mnmdrops.com/)** — a fan-run item and drop database, organized by zone and mob, with in-game item card screenshots.
- **[MNM Realm](https://mnmrealm.com/)** — a fan-made database covering items, spells, NPCs, zones, quests, classes, races, tradeskills, and recipes, aiming for the feel of classic MMO database sites like EQ Casters Realm and Allakhazam. Also mirrors official patch notes/news and takes community submissions through a Discord bot.
